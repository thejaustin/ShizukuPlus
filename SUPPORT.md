# Getting help with Shizuku+

- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/thejaustin/ShizukuPlus/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). The form asks which app you use (Shizuku+ or the Drop-In), your device and your version. A Doctor or Activity-log report from the app (Settings) gets it fixed much faster.
- **You'd like something added:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/thejaustin/ShizukuPlus/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Search first; someone may already have asked.
- **A question, or you're not sure it's a bug:** ask in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/ShizukuPlus), or check the [wiki](https://github.com/thejaustin/ShizukuPlus/wiki) first.
- **A security problem:** please don't post it publicly; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **You'd like to help:** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how, and issues labelled `good first issue` or `help wanted` are good places to start.

This is a hobby project, so replies can take a little while. We read everything.
